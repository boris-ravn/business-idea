# Customer Discovery: Interviews with Real Estate Agents in Cochabamba

*Three real interviews, conducted in Spanish in October 2026 and translated into English. The Spanish transcript is in [interviews-original-es.md](interviews-original-es.md). Audio recordings are not published to protect the participants' privacy; only first names are used.*

## Method

- **Goal:** understand how independent agents find properties for their clients and share their own listings today, and where they lose time, **before** proposing any solution.
- **Approach:** *The Mom Test*: questions about past behavior and concrete recent situations ("tell me about the last time…"), not opinions about a hypothetical product. No product was shown during these interviews.
- **Participants:** 3 agents from Cochabamba with different profiles (rentals vs. sales, with and without a strong network). One live call; two answered the questions by WhatsApp voice notes.
- **Transcription:** voice notes were transcribed locally with Whisper (`small` model), with minimal cleanup.
- **Earlier preparatory interviews** (simulated, used only to draft hypotheses) are not included and are not cited as evidence.

### Interview guide

1. Tell me about your experience as an agent. How many clients do you work with per month? What do you mainly focus on?
2. Tell me about the last time a client asked you for a property. How did you look for options? How long did it take to find a suitable one? What information was missing or did you have to confirm?
3. When you have a new property to offer, where and how do you share it, and what results does it usually give you?
4. Do you use anything to organize yourself, like a spreadsheet or notes for your properties and clients?
5. Do you currently pay for advertising or any platform?
6. Of your whole current workflow, what frustrates you the most, or where do you lose the most time?
7. When searching for a property, which categories or filters do you consider essential?

**Glossary of local terms**
- **Consigna:** a property listing an agent takes on from an owner (a listing mandate), with or without exclusivity.
- **Baulera:** a storage unit, usually in the basement of an apartment building.
- **Expensas:** monthly building or HOA maintenance fees.
- **Bolivianos (Bs):** Bolivian currency.
- **Colcapirhua, Sacaba:** municipalities in the Cochabamba metropolitan area.

## 1. María

**Interviewer:** Tell me a bit about your experience as an agent. How many clients do you work with per month? What do you mainly focus on?

**María:** I usually focus mostly on rentals. Sales too, yes, but fewer.

**Interviewer:** Tell me about the last time a client asked you for a property. How did you look for options? How long did it take you to find a suitable option? What information was missing or did you have to confirm?

**María:** To find the house I sold, for example, through the WhatsApp groups we have, the office ones and the outside agents' ones. That's how I found the property. We did the corresponding visits, and that time it took me more or less a week to close the house.

**Interviewer:** When you have a new property to offer, where and how do you share it, and what results does it usually give you?

**María:** When I have a new property, it's usually posted in the office groups, in the office system, as long as you have an exclusive.

**Interviewer:** What usually gives you results and tends to be efficient?

**María:** The ideal, and the most efficient way of working, is posting among agents so that, all together, a sale or a rental can happen much faster.

**Interviewer:** Do you use anything to organize yourself, like an Excel sheet or some notes to organize your properties and clients?

**María:** Yes, I use an Excel sheet where I put all the properties I've closed and that I'm going to post. Apart from that, I also have my own WhatsApp where I keep my posts organized so I can publish them, whether on Facebook, WhatsApp, Marketplace and everything that needs to be posted.

**Interviewer:** Do you currently pay for advertising or any platform?

**María:** I usually pay sometimes for TikTok, or also for Meta Business.

**Interviewer:** Of your whole current workflow, what would you say frustrates you the most, or where do you lose the most time?

**María:** Precisely finding the right property for the client sometimes, because sometimes the clients' budget is a bit low, you know, and searching in the WhatsApp groups [going through] everything further back takes you time. So that's the most complicated part. And also when other agents don't reply when you ask them something.

**Interviewer:** Finally, when searching for a property, which categories or filters do you consider essential?

**María:** For apartments, it's usually the matter of pets and parking, and sometimes a storage unit. For houses, people usually ask for more modern houses, if it's to live in.

## 2. Nicolás

*He answered the questions he received in writing in a single voice note. The headings show which question each part answers, based on the order and content of his answer.*

**Experience**

Well, I started working part-time in real estate back in high school, because my uncle has a real estate agency, and my family and I build gated communities of houses in Colcapirhua and Sacaba. So we offer our own properties as well as other people's properties. I personally focus on sales, but I did have experience in rentals too, at the beginning.

**The last time a client asked you for a property / how you looked for options**

Well, I get the options from my uncle's agency. He has the options; I just offer them through my sales channel, which is TikTok. It went viral for me and it has helped me a lot.

**When you have a new property, where and how do you share it?**

Then also, if I have a *consigna* [listing], we have a group of a thousand [?] real estate agencies. My uncle is actually the founder of that group, and I sometimes help him manage it: adding people, removing people or moderating, because maybe sometimes they fight and come back [?], that kind of thing. So I help him with that. That's my sales channel; it's a fast channel, let's say. We have a property, we offer it there, we work with other agencies and it sells fast or rents fast. And the same goes the other way: from there we get properties for any client who needs one. We send requirements and we find things easily.

**Do you use anything to organize yourself?**

To organize myself, I use a private group of my uncle's agency, and there we organize the properties we have available, the properties' spec sheets and the commissions.

**Do you pay for advertising or any platform?**

Yes, advertising is paid. My uncle pays for advertising depending on the type of listing: if it's an exclusive listing, meaning they give us exclusivity for a few months to sell a property, then yes, advertising is paid; if it's open, then no. Then, personally, as an independent agent let's say, I don't pay for advertising. What has helped me a lot is the TikTok that went viral [account name removed]; that has helped me sell. And I think my uncle put in about 200 bolivianos a month, depending on the property, on the sales.

**What frustrates you the most, or where do you lose the most time?**

Nothing frustrates us, but you could say... I don't know if it's frustration, but it's like part of the job: you go show [a property] to, say, 20 clients and sometimes you won't be the one to sell it, someone else will, another person from the same agency, let's say, within my uncle's agency.

Where the most time is lost, let's say, and what I like doing the least, is maybe the *consigna* [taking on the listing]: going to a property, taking photos, meeting with the owner, reaching the agreement. A bit tedious. Then time is lost, so to speak, going on visits with clients; sometimes those clients don't buy, like I'm telling you.

**Which filters or categories do you consider essential when searching for a property?**

The first thing you do... well, to search for properties there are actually several things. I'd maybe start with the number of rooms, because that's what people look for: "I have a family of five, so I want at least four bedrooms." That would help me filter. Then what I ask about is the area: "Do you have a preferred area?" The budget would also help a lot; maybe I'd make it the first filter, to know how much people are able to pay when it comes to sales. Then, whether it has basic utilities like water, gas, electricity, whether internet reaches it. Also whether the documentation, the paperwork, is in order. If you're going to build a website or something like that, it would be good to have a check, let's say, for the documentation. And whether bank financing is accepted, because in sales it often happens that not everyone pays cash.

## 3. Valeria

*She answered with one voice note per question. The headings show which question each voice note answers.*

**Experience** *(voice note 1, 0:26)*

Hi, good afternoon, Boris. I'm going to answer the questions you're asking me. I've been working in real estate for almost a year, since 2025. Before that I [have] worked independently as an agent and little by little I gained more experience. Currently I handle both rentals and sales of properties, and I serve approximately between 5 and 10 clients per month.

**The last time a client asked you for a property / how you looked for options** *(voice note 2, 0:21)*

Recently a client asked me for an apartment with a garage that allowed pets. I looked for options on Marketplace and in WhatsApp groups where we agents share properties, and it took me about two days to find alternatives, because I had to confirm prices, availability and some of the rental conditions.

**When you have a new property, where and how do you share it?** *(voice note 3, 0:16)*

I mainly post on Facebook Marketplace, because that's where I get the most interested people. I also belong to several WhatsApp groups with other real estate agents, where we share properties and work together to close rentals or sales.

**Do you use anything to organize yourself?** *(voice note 4, 0:14)*

I organize myself mainly with WhatsApp, phone notes and screenshots, since it works quite well for me. Although when I have several properties or clients at the same time, it gets a bit more complicated to find all the information quickly.

**Do you pay for advertising or any platform?** *(voice note 5, 0:15)*

I mostly use Marketplace for free, but sometimes I spend between 50 and 100 bolivianos to promote certain properties and reach more people. For now I don't pay for any real estate platform on a regular basis.

**What frustrates you the most, or where do you lose the most time?** *(voice note 6, 0:15)*

The most frustrating thing is when I have a client who is really interested in visiting a property and, when I ask, it turns out it's already been rented or sold. That makes me lose a lot of time, and many times [I lose] the opportunity [?] to develop a deal or to work the property as such.

**Which filters or categories do you consider essential when searching for a property?** *(voice note 7, 0:22)*

And lastly, I consider the filters for location, price, number of bedrooms, garage, pets, size and building fees (*expensas*) important. But for me the most useful thing would be for the properties to be up to date and for us to be able to verify their availability quickly, without having to ask several agents.
