## Moderator notes

- **Most important observation:** for rentals and *anticrético*, a listing has a lifecycle (rented → contract ends → available again). The prototype treats "closed" as final.
- **Best quote:** *"Saber cuándo se alquilaron, hasta cuándo…"* (Know when they were rented, until when…)
- **Bugs or confusing moments:** fields that don't apply when publishing; no way to reopen a closed listing.
- **Surprises:** a new need beyond the original hypotheses: managing the rental lifecycle of one's own listings.
