# Validation: moderated test with real agents

The first prototype feedback came from a heuristic walkthrough, not from real users. This folder documents the experiment that validates MVP v2 with **3 to 5 real agents in Cochabamba**.

**Status:** in progress (October 2026).

## What this test answers

1. **Search:** is finding a property with filters clearly better than going through WhatsApp groups?
2. **Publish:** is publishing by pasting their usual WhatsApp message easy enough that they'd do it? Does the parser understand real messages?
3. **Share:** do agents understand and use the two sharing modes (for my client / for agents)?
4. **Keep it fresh:** does "Confirm all" make keeping listings up to date easy, which is the riskiest assumption?

The success thresholds were set **before** the test, in [PRD v1 §12](../02-product/prd-v1-mvp.md) and [PRD v2 §7](../02-product/prd-v2-feedback-iteration.md).

## Privacy

Participants are identified as P1, P2… or by first name only. Their pasted messages are included only with their permission, and with no phone numbers or owners' personal data.
