# PRD v2: Prototype Iteration Based on Feedback

> **For the implementing agent:** this document describes **only what changes** relative to [`prd-v1-mvp.md`](prd-v1-mvp.md) (v1). Everything not mentioned here stays the same: stack, design principles, seed data, `DEMO_MODE`, local persistence, and what remains out of scope. It is still a **prototype for user testing**: no backend, no login, and no features beyond this scope.

- **Author:** Boris Martínez
- **Status:** Approved and implemented (October 20, 2026). Pending: moderated test with real agents (§7).
- **Date:** October 20, 2026
- **Scope of this iteration:** priorities **P0** and **P1**. P2 improvements are left out (see §6).

---

## 1. Context: where this feedback comes from

After the first demo, the prototype was tested on a phone following the 4 tasks of the test plan (§12 of v1). The feedback is consolidated in the internal analysis notes §1 and in [`business-plan.md`](../03-business/business-plan.md) §5.

**How to read this evidence:** the walkthrough was done by an AI agent acting as a user (a heuristic review), not by a real real-estate agent. That is why the changes in this document are treated as **improvement hypotheses**, not as validated needs. They will be validated in the next test with 3 to 5 real agents (see §7). Several points match what María, Nicolás and Valeria said in the real interviews, which gives them more weight. This is noted case by case.

### What worked (kept unchanged)
- Filtering is fast and the results counter helps.
- Pasting the message and having the listing fill itself in is a pleasant surprise.
- The freshness badge and the standard listing give information that WhatsApp does not.
- No need to create an account: a name and WhatsApp number are enough.

### What changes in this iteration

| ID | Priority | Feedback | Support in the real interviews |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | P0 | "If I share a property with my **client**, I don't want them to see the commission or that it belongs to another agent." | Valeria posts for clients (Marketplace) and for agents (WhatsApp groups): they are two different audiences |
| C2 | P0 | "Pasting my message surprised me, but then the review is too long: just show me what's missing." | — |
| C3 | P0 | "It didn't understand '4 mil Bs' (4 thousand Bs) or the zone when I wrote 'Av. Pando'. That's how we write here." | — |
| C4 | P0 | "It tells me 2 properties need confirmation, but which ones? Put them first or let me confirm them all at once." | Valeria: the biggest pain is that the property *"was already rented or sold"* |
| C5 | P1 | "The *expensas* (monthly building/HOA fees) are missing, and they matter for rentals." | Valeria mentions *expensas* as an essential filter |
| C6 | P1 | "If I search for 2 bedrooms, show me the 3-bedroom ones too." | Nicolás: *"I want at least four rooms"* |
| C7 | P1 | "The commission and whether the agent has the owner's authorization should be at the top, not at the end." | — |

**Why this order:** the P0 items directly target the riskiest assumptions of the product: that agents **publish by pasting** (C2, C3), that they **keep listings fresh** (C4), and that the tool fits how they share today (C1). The P1 items are search and listing-readability improvements that are cheap to implement.

## 2. P0 changes

### C1. Share in two modes: "Para asesores" (For agents) and "Para mi cliente" (For my client)

**Problem.** The text generated by "Compartir" (Share) (F6 in v1) always includes the shared commission and the owner's authorization. That works for agent groups, but the agent cannot forward it to their client without editing it by hand.

**Solution.**
- The "Compartir" (Share) sheet has a two-option selector above the text: **"Para asesores"** (For agents) and **"Para mi cliente"** (For my client). The preview text changes instantly.
- **Default mode:**
  - Listing of a property **from another agent** → "Para mi cliente" (most likely they will share it with their client).
  - **Own** property (My properties and the success screen after publishing) → "Para asesores".
- **"Para asesores"** stays as in v1 and adds the *expensas* for rentals (see C5).
- **"Para mi cliente"**:
  - Does **not** include the commission, the owner's authorization, the name of the agent who published it, or the link to the listing (in the prototype the link only opens on the same phone, and the listing shows internal data).
  - Includes a contact line with the **name and WhatsApp of the person sharing** (the locally saved profile).
  - Shows availability only if it is recent (green badge, ≤ 3 days): "✅ Disponible (confirmado hoy)" (✅ Available (confirmed today)). If it is not recent, that line does not appear; the client is not shown "Sin confirmar hace 20 días" (Not confirmed for 20 days).
  - If the person sharing does not have a profile yet, before showing the text they are asked for their name and WhatsApp using the same profile sheet that already exists when publishing.
- Example "Para mi cliente" text:
  ```
  🏠 Departamento en alquiler – Cala Cala
  💵 Bs 3.800 + Bs 250 de expensas · 2 dorm · 2 baños · 85 m²
  🚗 Garaje · 🐾 Acepta mascotas
  ✅ Disponible (confirmado hoy)
  📲 Más información: Carla Rojas · wa.me/59170000001
  ```
  English version:
  ```
  🏠 Apartment for rent – Cala Cala
  💵 Bs 3,800 + Bs 250 in expensas · 2 bed · 2 bath · 85 m²
  🚗 Garage · 🐾 Pets allowed
  ✅ Available (confirmed today)
  📲 More information: Carla Rojas · wa.me/59170000001
  ```
- **Measurement:** the `share_click` event adds the `mode` field (`agents` | `client`).

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [ ] On another agent's listing, "Compartir" opens in "Para mi cliente"; on an own property, it opens in "Para asesores".
- [ ] The "Para mi cliente" text does not contain the commission, "Autorizado por el propietario" (Authorized by the owner), the name of the agent who published it, or the link to the listing.
- [ ] The "Para mi cliente" text ends with the name and WhatsApp from the sharer's profile. Without a profile, it is requested first.
- [ ] A property with the amber or gray badge does not show the availability line in client mode.
- [ ] "Copiar texto" (Copy text) and "Abrir WhatsApp" (Open WhatsApp) use the text of the selected mode, and `share_click` records `mode`.
- [ ] Unit tests of `shareText` for both modes.

### C2. Compact review when publishing: what's missing first

**Problem.** In step 2 of Publish ("Revisar" (Review)) all fields are shown, detected and missing ones mixed together. Even if the parser detected almost everything, the agent has to go through a long screen.

**Solution.**
- The "Revisar" (Review) step is split into three parts, in this order:
  1. **Summary of what was detected:** a card with the detected data in one or two lines of text (for example: "Departamento · Alquiler · Cala Cala · Bs 3.500 · 2 dorm · 1 baño · Garaje · Mascotas · Comparte 50%" (Apartment · Rent · Cala Cala · Bs 3,500 · 2 bed · 1 bath · Garage · Pets · Shares 50%)), the count "Detectamos 9 de 12 datos" (We detected 9 of 12 fields) and an **"Editar"** (Edit) link that expands the detected fields so they can be corrected.
  2. **"Te falta"** (You're missing): only the relevant fields that were not detected, with the same controls as today (chips and toggles). The required ones (operation, type, zone and price) go first and are marked as required. If nothing is missing: "¡Ficha completa!" (Listing complete!).
  3. **Always visible:** the "Tengo autorización del propietario" (I have the owner's authorization) checkbox (the parser does not detect it) and the photos, in a compact row.
- If the agent chose "Prefiero llenarlo manualmente" (I'd rather fill it in manually), nothing is detected: all fields appear under "Te falta", as today.
- When the agent completes a field in "Te falta", the field **stays in place** until they leave the step (it does not jump to the summary while they type).
- **Measurement:** new `publish_review_expanded` event when "Editar" is opened. `publish_completed` adds `missingAtStart` (how many fields were missing on arriving at the step).

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [ ] With the Cala Cala rental seed message, on a 360×800 viewport the summary and the first "Te falta" field are visible without scrolling.
- [ ] Detected fields do not appear as controls until "Editar" is tapped. When they are edited, the summary updates.
- [ ] If a required field is missing, the "Publicar" (Publish) button flags it the same as today.
- [ ] The manual flow still works.

### C3. Parser with local language

**Problem.** The parser does not understand how prices and locations are written in Cochabamba.

**Solution.**
- **Prices with "mil" (thousand):** recognize `4 mil`, `4mil`, `3,5 mil`, `3.5 mil`, `Bs 4 mil`, `4 mil Bs`, `95 mil $us` and `$us 95 mil`, just as `95k` is recognized today. "Mil" multiplies by 1,000.
- **Zone variants:** recognize zones even when written with a hyphen or joined: `Queru-Queru`, `Queruqueru`, `Cala-Cala`, `Calacala`.
- **Known avenues and places:** a `ZONE_ALIASES` dictionary that maps avenues and places to a zone on the list. Rules:
  - If the message names a zone on the list, the zone wins.
  - If it only names an avenue or place from the dictionary, its zone is suggested. In the review it is marked as **"Zona sugerida por 'Av. Pando': revísala"** (Zone suggested from 'Av. Pando': check it), not as "detected", because an avenue can cross several zones.
  - The variants `Av.`, `Avda.`, `Avenida` and no prefix are recognized.
- **Dictionary contents:** Boris defines and validates it before implementation (see §8). Candidate places: Av. Pando, Av. América, Av. Santa Cruz, Av. Ayacucho, Av. Heroínas, Av. Villazón, Av. Blanco Galindo, Av. Melchor Pérez de Olguín, Av. Circunvalación, El Prado, Plaza Colón, Plaza 14 de Septiembre, Laguna Alalay and Cristo de la Concordia.
- ***Expensas*:** in addition to the current format (`expensas 250`), recognize `250 de expensas`, `+ 250 expensas` and `expensas incluidas` (= 0). See also the C5 bug.
- The `parseListing(text)` signature does not change. The result may include `suggested: string[]` for suggested fields (today only the zone).

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [ ] New parser tests for each "mil" format, the zone variants, at least 3 dictionary avenues and the *expensas* formats.
- [ ] "Alquilo depto 2 dorm por Av. Pando, 4 mil Bs" (Renting out 2-bed apartment near Av. Pando, 4 thousand Bs) detects operation, type, bedrooms, price = 4,000 Bs and suggests the zone associated with Av. Pando.
- [ ] The 8 seed messages still detect the same as before (no regressions).
- [ ] 3 new seed messages that use "mil" and avenues are added.

### C4. Confirm availability: which ones, and all at once

**Problem.** In My properties, the banner says "2 propiedades necesitan confirmación" (2 properties need confirmation), but it doesn't say which ones, and they have to be confirmed one by one. Keeping listings fresh is the riskiest assumption of the product: if confirming takes effort, agents won't do it.

**Solution.**
- On entering My properties (Active tab), **the ones that need confirmation come first**, sorted by most days without confirmation. The order is calculated **on entry** and does not change while the agent confirms, so the cards don't jump.
- The banner names the properties when there are 3 or fewer ("Depto en Cala Cala y Casa en Sarco necesitan confirmación" (Apartment in Cala Cala and House in Sarco need confirmation)) and shows the number when there are more.
- The banner has the **"Confirmar todas (N)"** (Confirm all (N)) button. When tapped, all of them become "Confirmado hoy" (Confirmed today) and a toast "N propiedades confirmadas hoy" (N properties confirmed today) appears with **"Deshacer"** (Undo), which restores each one's previous date.
- Confirming one by one still works as today.
- **Measurement:** `confirm_available` adds `bulk: true | false`. Bulk confirmation records a single event with `count`.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [ ] With the seed data, own properties with more than 7 days without confirmation appear first in Active.
- [ ] "Confirmar todas" turns all pending ones green, the banner disappears, and "Deshacer" returns them to their previous state.
- [ ] Confirming a single one does not reorder the list.
- [ ] Reducer tests for bulk confirmation and undo.

## 3. P1 changes

### C5. *Expensas* for rentals

**Problem.** The model already has `rentExpenses` and the listing already shows them, but:
- **Bug:** the parser detects the *expensas*, and `draftToListing` discards them (it always saves them as `null`).
- The publish review has no *expensas* field.
- The list card does not show them, so the real monthly cost is not visible.

**Solution.**
- Add `rentExpenses` to the publish draft and keep the detected value.
- In the review (rentals only), an **Expensas** field with the options "Incluidas" (Included) (= 0) or an amount in Bs. It counts as a relevant field for "Te falta" (C2).
- On the rental card, next to the price: "Bs 3.800 + 250 exp." (Bs 3,800 + 250 fees). If included: "Bs 3.800 (exp. incluidas)" (Bs 3,800 (fees included)). Without data, only the price is shown.
- The share texts (C1) include the *expensas* for rentals.
- No *expensas* filter is added in this iteration.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [ ] Publishing a message with the *expensas* amount (for example, "expensas Bs 250") saves the amount and it shows on the card, on the listing and in the share text.
- [ ] The Expensas field appears only for rentals.
- [ ] A `draftToListing` test that covers *expensas*.

### C6. Bedrooms as a minimum ("2 or more")

**Problem.** The bedrooms filter is exact: searching for 2 hides the 3-bedroom ones.

**Solution.**
- The chips become **"1+", "2+", "3+" and "4+"**, meaning "at least N bedrooms".
- The chip label in the filter bar reads "2+ dorm" (2+ bed).
- The empty state and the most-restrictive-filter suggestion keep working.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [ ] "2+" shows properties with 2, 3, 4 or more bedrooms and excludes those without the data.
- [ ] `matches` tests updated.
- [ ] The v1 acceptance criterion that uses the bedrooms filter is updated.

### C7. Collaboration higher up in the listing

**Problem.** The shared commission and the owner's authorization are the first things an agent wants to know, and today they are in the 5th block of the listing.

**Solution.**
- New block order in the listing (F2 in v1): **Price and operation → Availability → Collaboration → Features → Terms → Agent → Description.**
- The content of each block does not change.

**Acceptance criteria.**
- [ ] On the listing, the Collaboration block appears immediately after Availability.

1. **Low-fi wireframes first** (*wireframer* skill, `AGENTS.md` rules), only for the screens that change:
   - "Compartir" (Share) sheet with the two modes (C1).

   5. C4: bulk confirmation.
   6. C6 and C7.
3. Verify each change on a 360×800 viewport before moving to the next one. Run all tests and the build at the end.

## 7. Updated test plan (run by Boris)

Same as in v1 (3 to 5 agents, about 15 minutes, on their phone, thinking aloud), with these tasks:

1. "Your client is looking for an apartment for rent, **with 2 or more bedrooms**, with a garage and that allows pets, up to 4,000 Bs. Find options and **send one to your client**." *(validates C1 and C6)*
2. "Publish one of your properties: copy the last message you sent to a group and paste it here." *(validates C2, C3 and C5)*
3. "You have properties you haven't confirmed in days. Bring them up to date." *(validates C4)*
4. "One of your properties has already been rented. Update it."
5. "Share one of your properties in your agents group." *(validates C1)*

**Criteria defined in advance** (the v1 criteria are kept and these are added):

| Signal | Continue | Revisit the change |
|---|---|---|
| Task 1: they choose or accept "Para mi cliente" without help | ≥ 60% | < 40% |
| Task 2: they complete the review without opening "Editar" | ≥ 50% | They open "Editar" almost always → the parser gets it wrong too often |
| Task 3: they use "Confirmar todas" | ≥ 60% | They ignore it or don't find the pending ones |
| The parser detects ≥ 5 fields with their real message (from v1) | ≥ 60% of messages | < 30% → evaluate a real LLM |

**Additional closing question:** "Would you send that text to your client as is? What would you change?"
